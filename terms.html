2. AI text recognition (manual scanning and odometer photos)

“Scan your manual” uses Tesseract, an open-source AI text-recognition (OCR) model, together with pattern matching, on your device. Its results are machine-generated, are labelled as such in the app, and may contain errors — for example a misread number or an interval matched to the wrong part. You must check every value against your manual before saving it. Use “Report a problem” to tell us about a wrong result.

“Read it from a photo” in Update mileage reads the odometer numbers from a photo using the same Tesseract model, plus BreonMoto’s own seven-segment digit reader for LCD displays. It can misread digits, or pick up the speed, trip meter or clock instead of the odometer. The number it suggests is machine-generated: check it against your odometer before saving. The photo is read on your device and is not stored or uploaded.
