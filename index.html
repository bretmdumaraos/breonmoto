Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is BreonMoto really free for unlimited vehicles?

Yes. Add as many motorcycles or cars as you need. Maintenance tools, fuel tracking, the trip planner, reports, backups, and sync are all free. The Google Play version displays one small banner ad.

What does “Remove ads” include?

It is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play that turns off the Google AdMob banner. There is no subscription and no extra feature tier.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

You do not need a BreonMoto account to track vehicles locally. Internet is used for Google Play billing, ads, Google Maps, and optional sync. Google sign-in is needed only if you choose Drive sync.

Where is my vehicle data stored?

Vehicle details and records are saved on your device by default. When you enable sync, BreonMoto copies the relevant records and files into a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive for your other Android devices. BreonMoto does not operate a server that receives your records.

Does BreonMoto scan my odometer and receipts online?

No. Odometer, receipt and owner’s manual recognition are performed on your device. Review any recognised numbers before saving. If you enable sync, saved records and related photos can then be copied to your Google Drive.

Does the app show ads or use analytics?

The Google Play version shows one small Google AdMob banner on certain screens unless you remove ads. Google’s SDK may collect ad-related device data. BreonMoto does not use its own analytics. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Does BreonMoto support Filipino?

Yes. The app and full user guide support English and Filipino (Tagalog).

Are the recommended service dates guaranteed?

No. They are estimates based on mileage, dates, and available guidance. Check your owner’s manual and consult a qualified mechanic, especially for safety-related parts.
