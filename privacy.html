Privacy policy

See what stays on your device, how optional Google Drive sync works, and how Google AdMob handles ads.

Effective 13 October 2026

In short: your vehicles, logs, parts, tyre photos and manuals stay on your device. There are no accounts and no analytics, and nothing you enter is uploaded to us. The Google Play version of the app shows one small banner ad from Google AdMob; you can remove it with a one-time purchase. The only things that can leave your device are listed below, and each happens only when you choose it.

Who we are

BreonMoto is made by Bret Michael Dumaraos, an individual developer in the Philippines (“we”, “us”). Contact: bretmdumaraos@gmail.com.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored in the app’s private storage on your device: vehicle details (type, make, model, year, nickname, plate number if you add one, odometer readings), papers (registration, insurance and emission-test dates, insurer name, your driver’s licence expiry date), receipt photos and shop names, pre-ride checks and tyre-pressure readings, maintenance and performance logs (dates, mileage, costs, notes, fill-ups, litres and fuel prices), parts and their intervals, tyre checks (readings, DOT date codes and photos you take), and the text read from owner’s manuals you scan. It stays there until you delete it in the app, use “Delete all data”, or uninstall the app.

Manual scanning reads PDFs and photos of your owner’s manual on your device, using an open-source text-recognition (OCR) model built into the app. The files and pictures are not sent anywhere. Only the recognised text is kept, and only if you save the result.

Photos of your vehicle and tyres are taken with your phone’s camera app or chosen with Android’s photo picker, so BreonMoto sees only the pictures you pick or take. They are stored only in the app. A photo you take to read your odometer is read on the device and then discarded; only the number you save is kept.

Home-screen widget and notifications show the next items due (for example “Engine oil — due in 5 days”) on your own screen. Anyone who can see your screen can see them; you can remove the widget or turn reminders off at any time.

Backups and exports, including the service-history PDF, are files you save yourself — to your Gallery, a folder you choose, or another app through Share. Where they go from there is up to you.

What can leave your device

WhenWhat is sent, and to whom
If you send “Report a problem”What you type, the category you choose, the context line if you leave it ticked (for example the manual’s name or a tyre-check result), and the app version. It is sent as an e-mail to our support address through your own e-mail app, so you see the message before you send it, and your e-mail provider handles it in transit. We use it only to fix the problem and delete it within 12 months.
Banner ad (Google Play version, unless you removed ads)One small banner from Google AdMob is shown at the bottom of the garage and vehicle screens. To show it, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK collects and uses information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app details, and how you interact with the ad. This data goes directly between your device and Google; we do not receive it, and we do not send Google anything you enter in BreonMoto (vehicles, logs, photos, odometer). Whether ads are personalised depends on your Android ad settings and, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other places, on the choice you make in Google’s consent form. You can change that choice at any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices (shown where it applies). You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Buying “Remove ads” stops ads, and the ad SDK is then not asked for any ad.
If you buy “Remove ads”Payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google tells the app whether you own it. Google’s privacy policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy.
If you use Share or save a fileThe file goes to the app or place you pick; that app’s privacy policy applies from then on.
If you use the trip plannerWhen you tap “Open Google Maps”, the Google Maps app opens and uses your location under Google’s own settings. The destination you typed, if any, is passed to the Google Maps app or website, under Google’s own privacy policy. This happens only when you tap the button. BreonMoto does not track your location.
If you turn on sync (Android)You sign in with your Google account through Google Play services. BreonMoto then saves a copy of your vehicles, logs, parts, settings, photos and manual pages in a hidden BreonMoto folder in your own Google Drive, so your other devices signed in to the same account can use them. BreonMoto can only see that one folder, not the rest of your Drive. The data goes directly between your device and Google; we have no server and never receive it. It uses your Drive storage and Google’s privacy policy applies. You can turn sync off at any time, and Delete from Google Drive in Settings removes the copy (you can also remove BreonMoto’s access in your Google Account settings).
If you tap a source linkThe page opens in your browser, under that website’s own privacy policy.

We do not sell personal data, use analytics, or build profiles. The only third party that receives data through the app is Google, for the banner ad, for Google Play billing and, if you turn it on, for sync and Maps as described above. We have no server that receives your data.

Children

BreonMoto is meant for adult vehicle owners and is not directed at children. Ads are limited to mild content (Google’s “PG” maximum content rating).

Your choices and rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly: edit or delete any entry, delete a vehicle, or use Settings › Delete all data. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. If you sent us a report and want it deleted sooner, e-mail us.

If you are in the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 gives you rights over personal information; contact us to exercise them. You can also contact the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, and mention the change in the app’s release notes.
